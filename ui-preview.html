Component 08

Form inputs New

No inputs exist on the site today. Font size is 16px minimum — anything smaller makes iOS Safari zoom on focus and yank the layout sideways. Hints sit above the field, never below.

Component 09

Event detail boxes In use

Native <details> — no JS, keyboard accessible for free. Never collapse essentials: date, time, venue and price stay visible. Only parking, FAQ and policies go behind a disclosure.
